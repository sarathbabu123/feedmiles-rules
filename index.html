Nothing leaves your phone

  • FeedMiles keeps numbers only: distance, plus counts of posts, reels and ads.
  • It never stores usernames, captions, photos, videos, messages, links or anything you type.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads. The only thing it downloads is a small, signed rules file that helps it recognise labels like "Sponsored".
  • It only looks at Instagram and YouTube, and only while they're open. You can pause it or delete everything in one tap.

How it works

FeedMiles uses Android's Accessibility service to see how far the screen scrolls and which labels the apps already show, such as "Sponsored" and "Suggested for you". It converts on-screen pixels into real distance using your screen's density. Because a flick sends content flying, feed distance is more than your thumb moved, and we say so. Anything it can't classify shows as Unknown and is never guessed.
