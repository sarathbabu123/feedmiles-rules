Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

In short: FeedMiles keeps numbers only (distances and counts), on your phone. It never sends anything about you anywhere. No account, no analytics, no ads.

1. Who we are

FeedMiles is an Android app made by an independent developer in India. Questions or requests: sarathbabuparakkadavu@gmail.com.

2. What the app measures and why

FeedMiles shows you how far you scroll in Instagram and YouTube each day, and how much of it was people you follow, suggested posts from strangers, and ads.

To do this it uses Android's AccessibilityService API. Only while Instagram or YouTube is open, it reads how far the screen scrolls and the labels those apps already show, such as "Sponsored" and "Suggested for you". It uses these in memory to decide which category the content belongs to, then discards them.

It does not read any other app. It never reads messages, password fields, anything you type, or notifications. It never taps or acts for you.

Never stored: usernames, captions, comments, hashtags, photos, videos, thumbnails, audio, messages, links, account IDs, contacts or location.

The data is kept in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

4. What leaves your phone

Nothing about you. The app makes one kind of network request: about once a day it downloads a small public "rules" file that tells it how to recognise labels like "Sponsored". That request sends nothing but a standard web request. The file is digitally signed, and the app ignores it if the signature doesn't match.

Share cards (images of your numbers) go to another app only when you tap Share and pick where they go. They never include usernames, posts or anything you saw.

FeedMiles contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. If Google Play is how you installed it, Google may collect crash reports under its own policy.

5. Your controls

  • Pause tracking for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you resume (Settings).
  • Export your data as CSV or JSON (Settings → Privacy centre).
  • Delete everything in one tap (Settings → Privacy centre). Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.
  • Turn it off anytime: Settings → Accessibility → FeedMiles scroll tracking.

6. Children

FeedMiles is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending any data off your phone, it will ask for your permission first.

FeedMiles is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google.
