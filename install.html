Install FeedMiles

FeedMiles isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a few extra warnings. This takes about 2 minutes. You need Android 9 or newer.

  1. Download the app

    Download FeedMiles.apk

    Open the file when the download finishes. If someone sent it to you on WhatsApp, tap the file in the chat.

  2. If Play Protect blocks it

    In India, Google Play Protect blocks apps from outside the Play Store that use Accessibility, even safe ones. If you see "Blocked by Play Protect" or "App blocked to protect your device":

    Play Store → your profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙ (top right) → turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect"

    Then open the APK again and install.

    Turn Play Protect back on afterwards (same switch). FeedMiles keeps working.

  3. Allow the install

    Android may say your browser or WhatsApp "isn't allowed to install unknown apps". Tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back → Install.

  4. Switch on tracking

    Open FeedMiles, read what it does and doesn't keep, and tap I agree. It opens Accessibility settings: find FeedMiles scroll tracking and switch it on.

    If the switch is greyed out or you see "Restricted setting", that's Android being careful with apps from outside the Play Store. Do this once:

    • Try to switch it on once and close the warning.
    • Open Settings → Apps → FeedMiles.
    • Tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings and confirm with your PIN.
    • Go back to Accessibility and switch FeedMiles on.

    On some Samsung phones it's under Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps.

  5. Stop your phone from killing it

    Some phones turn off background apps to save battery, and tracking stops with them. FeedMiles uses under 1% battery a day. Find your phone below:

    Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO (HyperOS / MIUI)
    • Settings → Apps → Manage apps → FeedMiles → Autostart: on.
    • Same page → Battery saver → No restrictions.
    • Open recent apps, long-press FeedMiles and tap the lock icon.
    Samsung (One UI)
    • Settings → Apps → FeedMiles → Battery → Unrestricted.
    • Settings → Battery → Background usage limits: make sure FeedMiles isn't in "Sleeping apps" or "Deep sleeping apps".
    Oppo, Realme (ColorOS / Realme UI)
    • Settings → Apps → App management → FeedMiles → Battery usage: allow background activity and auto launch.
    • Lock FeedMiles in recent apps (pull the card down or tap the lock).
    Vivo, iQOO (Funtouch OS / OriginOS)
    • Settings → Battery → Background power consumption management → FeedMiles → Allow.
    • Settings → Apps → Autostart: turn FeedMiles on.
    OnePlus (OxygenOS)
    • Settings → Apps → FeedMiles → Battery usage → Allow background activity, and set it to Don't optimise.
    • Lock FeedMiles in recent apps.
    Pixel and others
    • Settings → Apps → FeedMiles → App battery usage → Unrestricted.

    Still not working? Turn FeedMiles off and on again in Accessibility.

  6. Go scroll

    Scroll Instagram or YouTube for a minute, then open FeedMiles to see how far your feed travelled.

Is this safe?

Android warns about every app from outside the Play Store, and Accessibility is a powerful permission, so it's right to ask. FeedMiles only reads Instagram and YouTube, keeps only numbers, and never sends anything about you off the phone. Read the privacy policy. You can switch it off anytime in Settings → Accessibility, or pause it inside the app.

FeedMiles is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google.
